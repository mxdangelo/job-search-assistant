# Preferences — how the CV is written

The facts live in `facts/`. This file says how to present them. The selection step reads
both.

## Numbers

Only from the fact base, only with `status: measured` or `stated`. A number says an
outcome (before → after) or a scale the reader can weigh, never a count of activity.

## Voice

- Result first, method after. Two lines for recent roles, one for old ones.
- No first person. No adjectives about yourself. No self-ratings.
- At most one "X, not Y" per CV.
- Reuse the posting's words for tools and skills, never its sentences.

## Length

One page by default. Two pages only when the posting's keywords and projects need the
room, and then the second page is full or absent.

## Chronology

Experiences are not deleted, they are compressed to one line: a deleted job leaves a gap,
and the gap is the first thing a recruiter checks.
